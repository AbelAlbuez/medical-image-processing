## Prose Finding

The four regimes form a negative-result chain. Intensity fails because the irreducible confounds are among the brightest enhancing structures. Location fails because those same confounds are not spatial outliers in the atlas; they sit at high atlas percentiles. Shape-proxy geometry partially separates components in isolation, but the deployable soft prior remains post-hoc and leaves high residual flood. Genuine cubical persistence fails most decisively: confounds are more persistent than true ET on the enhancement scalar. Together, the regimes support the paper-safe conclusion that no tested regime operating only on the enhancement scalar discriminates tumor from hard post-treatment confound reliably.
